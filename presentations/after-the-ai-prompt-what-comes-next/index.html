After the AI Prompt: What Comes Next

April 15, 2026
Presented by Marcelo Lewin
OC Merage Jewish Community Center • In-Person

About the Presentation

Ascend GenAI has put together a great event. See details below!

AI is evolving quickly, but most conversations are still focused on prompts, tools, and surface-level use cases.

This event is about what comes next.

We are bringing together founders, engineers, and builders who are working directly with generative AI in real-world settings from building companies to designing systems to deploying models.

Instead of focusing on what AI could do, this event focuses on what is actually happening now.

Key Topics Covered

  • How AI is shaping companies and industries
  • How AI systems and workflows are being built
  • How models are applied in real-world environments

About the Presenter

Marcelo Lewin, Founder @ iBuildWith.ai

Marcelo is the founder of iBuildWith.ai and a Principal Content Architect and Builder at Cigna. He has over 30 years of experience in the tech industry. Having lived through the computer, internet, and mobile revolutions, he is now excited and focused on navigating the AI revolution by helping non-developers build real applications using AI. He founded several startups and held roles at various companies including Toyota, NBC, J.F. Shea, and Walt Disney Imagineering.
