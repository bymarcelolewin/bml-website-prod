Presentation

An Intro to Gen UI for Adaptive Experiences

August 27, 2026
Presented by Jonathan Lewin
I Build with AI Meetup • In-Person

About the Presentation

AI experiences are moving beyond simple chat interfaces. Instead of only returning text, modern AI products can generate, assemble, or adapt interface elements based on what the user is trying to do.

In this presentation, Jonathan Lewin will introduce the concept of Generative UI, or Gen UI, and explore how it applies to adaptive AI experiences. He’ll show how AI can return cards, tables, forms, charts, workflows, and interactive components instead of long text responses. He’ll also cover the key patterns and technologies behind this shift, including tool calling, structured outputs, MCP, component catalogs, design systems, and SDKs such as Vercel AI SDK and OpenAI Apps SDK.

The goal is to give builders, designers, and product thinkers a practical understanding of how Gen UI works, why chat is not always the right interface, and how to start thinking about AI-native experiences that are more useful, contextual, and actionable.

In this workshop, Jonathan Lewin will demo an already-built app that uses Generative UI to create an adaptive AI experience. He’ll show how the app uses AI to return interactive components, adapt the interface to the user’s intent, and connect Gen UI patterns to tools, structured outputs, and reusable UI components.

About the Presenter

Jonathan Lewin, Cybersecurity Specialist @ Digicentric

Jonathan Lewin is a Cybersecurity Specialist at Digicentric with over three years of experience in the MSP space. He focuses on streamlining IT solutions with compliance at the core, handling everything from incident response to cutting through SIEM noise. Jonathan leverages AI at every step, developing standalone solutions and automations that keep cybersecurity front and center.
