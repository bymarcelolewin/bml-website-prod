Coding with AI Podcast

Conversations with humans building digital products with AI

Join me as I have conversations with humans building digital products with AI. From vibe coders and citizen developers to professional engineers and tool creators, we explore the tools, workflows, and real-world stories behind AI-assisted creation. Whether you're spinning up MVPs, experimenting with new ideas, or helping others build faster with AI, this podcast dives into all things vibe coding—how it's done, why it matters, and where it's headed. If you're curious about the future of creativity, code, and collaboration, this show is for you.
