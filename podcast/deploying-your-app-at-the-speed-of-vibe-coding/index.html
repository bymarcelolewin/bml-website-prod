Deploying Your App at the Speed of Vibe Coding

A conversation with Josh Thornes, Director of AI and Software Engineering @ e360

September 4, 2025

In this episode, we dive deep into the practical side of how to deploy your app into production. Join Marcelo and his guest, Josh Thornes, Director of AI and Software Engineering at e360, as they cover the best platforms for deployment, setting up seamless CI/CD pipelines, overcoming common deployment hurdles, and ensuring your vibe-coded apps are secure, reliable, and ready to scale.

Key Topics Covered

  1. Choosing the best deployment platforms for vibe-coded apps.
  2. Setting up efficient CI/CD pipelines for rapid iterations.
  3. Overcoming common deployment challenges unique to AI-generated prototypes.
  4. Ensuring security, reliability, and scalability from prototype to production.
  5. Monitoring tools and best practices to maintain your vibe-coded apps post-launch.

About the Guest

Josh Thornes, Director of AI and Software Engineering @ e360

Josh Thornes is a technology leader specializing in Generative AI, software architecture, and automation. With extensive AWS and Azure certifications, he helps companies at every stage of their SaaS journey—designing intelligent, scalable systems that accelerate development and enable data-driven insights.

About the Host

Marcelo Lewin, Founder @ iBuildWith.ai

Marcelo is the founder of iBuildWith.ai and a Principal Content Architect and Builder at Cigna. He has over 30 years of experience in the tech industry. Having lived through the computer, internet, and mobile revolutions, he is now excited and focused on navigating the AI revolution by helping non-developers build real applications using AI. He founded several startups and held roles at various companies including Toyota, NBC, J.F. Shea, and Walt Disney Imagineering.
